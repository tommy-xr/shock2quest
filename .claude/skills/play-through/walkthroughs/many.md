# Body of the Many (`many.mis`)

Source route: [GameBanshee's Body of the Many walkthrough](https://www.gamebanshee.com/systemshock2/walkthrough/bodyofthemany.php). Use this to review intended progression, not as a scripted set of moves. Confirm exact triggers and positions against the stock mission data and runtime.

The escape pod lands inside the Many. Break the membrane ahead to reach the first junction. The first nerve cluster hangs over a water pool beyond a steep slope and membrane. The route then passes another junction, irradiated swimming passages and a blowhole, then the second cluster. The path through watery tunnels, Rickenbacker wreckage and a blue nerve marked side passage reaches a sphincter that requires the two clusters.

Beyond that sphincter, the teeth room requires jumping between teeth and climbing blue nerve ladders. Destroy the cluster at the top to open the way onward. Farther ahead, break another membrane and drop into a deep pool. Swim out onto the bank and reach the Brain chamber through the remaining passage.

In the Brain fight, destroy the five Brain Structures around the chamber (three left, two right), then the orbiting defenders, then the central Brain. Climb the remaining stem and enter its opening; the current carries the player toward SHODAN.
