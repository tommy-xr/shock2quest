# SHODAN finale (`shodan.mis`)

Source route: [GameBanshee's Where Am I? walkthrough](https://www.gamebanshee.com/systemshock2/walkthrough/whereami.php). Check authored triggers and runtime state while playing.

From the Rickenbacker bridge, pass the final upgrade stations and follow SHODAN's reconstructed Citadel Station corridors. Delacroix's logs explain the throne room terminals. Traverse the ledges down to the final passage, enter the arena, and confirm the boss starts through normal entry.

The central face is shielded while a virtual SHODAN form attacks. The three authored Shield_Interlock CPUs (CPU1, CPU2, CPU3) lower the shield; then player weapon damage should destroy SHODAN and reach the ending. Verify terminal interaction in VR, shield state, avatar respawn and attacks, floor hazards, player survival or death, central boss damage, ending scene, and final game state. Do not count debug messages or direct level transition as a successful fight.
